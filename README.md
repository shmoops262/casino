# Neon Mirage Casino

Step into a retro 90's Miami-inspired web casino packed with neon vibes and interactive mini-games. Try your luck on slots, blackjack, roulette, and the Miami Dice table while tracking your winnings in the VIP lounge.

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your favorite web browser.
2. Click "Step into the Mirage" to jump to the gaming floor.
3. Use the navigation chips to switch between games and the VIP lounge.

## Games

- **Electric Palms Slots** – Spin the reels, place your bet, and chase jackpots with Miami icons.
- **Vice City Blackjack** – Classic blackjack with simplified rules and stylized cards.
- **Sunset Roulette** – Pick your bet type, spin the neon wheel, and watch the winnings roll in.
- **Miami Dice** – Roll the dice for natural wins, doubles, or hope to avoid snake eyes.
- **VIP Lounge** – Track your chip count, lifetime winnings, hot streak, and read the event log.

## Features

- Fully client-side experience with no build tools required.
- Synthwave-inspired design using Google Fonts and neon gradients.
- Persistent event log within the current session capturing every move you make.
- Chip, winnings, and streak tracking shared across all games.
